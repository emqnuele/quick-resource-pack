## Quick Resource Pack 2.0

A full rewrite. Same idea, much more of it.

**New**
- **Pack Wheel:** hold R to pick a pack from a wheel, or bind a key that opens it.
- **Slots:** as many packs as you like, each with its own key, including Shift, Ctrl and Alt combos.
- **Three modes:** Toggle (add on top), Profile (swap your whole pack list), Cycle (step through packs).
- **Rules:** turn slots on by themselves on a server, in a world or in a dimension.
- **No loading screen:** packs load in the background while you keep playing, with a progress toast.
- **New menu** in Minecraft's own style, also reachable from a button on the Resource Packs screen.
- **Commands:** `/qrp`, `/qrp toggle <slot>`, `/qrp wheel`, `/qrp list`, `/qrp reload`, `/qrp where`.
- **Optional on-screen pack icons**, like status effects.

**Changed**
- Cloth Config is no longer needed. Mod Menu is optional.
- Your 1.x pack, key and settings are moved over automatically.
- Keys no longer fire while you hold F3 for a debug shortcut.

**Requires** Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader and Fabric API. Versions for 26.1 and 26.2 are on the way; until then 1.2.0 (26.2) and 1.1.1 (26.1, 1.21.x) keep working.
